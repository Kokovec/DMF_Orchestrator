        Finalise 
        
    
        Finalise archives the workload, then deletes it. Into .state/archive/<id>-<time>/ it copies
      the container logs, SwitchFrame's state, the design, its Kubernetes resources and events, the source XML and
      compiled config, and the recordings, and checks the archive before anything is deleted.
        
    
        Then its volumes and its secrets are deleted, with its namespace, Flow Domain, allocation and
      generated files. That can't be undone: redeploying from the archive gives a new start, and asks for the secrets again.
        
    
        The facility must stay running until it ends. sudo asks for your password in the terminal.
